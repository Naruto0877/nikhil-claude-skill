Keep its instruction body aligned with [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) and [skills/nikhil-claude-skill/SKILL.md](skills/nikhil-claude-skill/SKILL.md). The root [README.md](README.md) describes the separate Claude and Codex skill installations.
